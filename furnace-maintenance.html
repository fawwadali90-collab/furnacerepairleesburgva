Furnace Maintenance

The Measurements That Matter: Temperature Rise and Static Pressure

A real tune-up is not a quick look and a filter change. The technician measures temperature rise across the heat exchanger and static pressure in the duct system, then compares both to the furnace nameplate. Those two numbers reveal whether the furnace is breathing right and heating right.

Temperature rise too high means restricted airflow cooking the heat exchanger. Too low means the furnace is not transferring heat properly. Either way, the numbers point to the fix instead of guesswork.

What Gets Cleaned, Tested, and Tightened

Flame sensors get cleaned, burners get inspected, the condensate drain gets cleared, electrical connections get tightened, capacitors get tested under load, and the blower wheel gets checked for buildup. Each is a small task that prevents a specific failure mode.

The visit ends with a full heating cycle test and a plain-language report: what is healthy, what is wearing, and what to watch. No scare tactics, just the condition of your equipment.

Fall Timing Beats January Regret

Loudoun County furnaces sit idle from April through October, then get asked for full output on the first cold snap. That first hard run is when weak igniters crack, old capacitors fail, and neglected vents reveal themselves.

Scheduling maintenance in September or October means any worn part is found on your schedule, not at midnight in January. It is the cheapest reliability your furnace can buy.

Common Furnace Maintenance Questions, Answered

What Happens During a Furnace Safety Inspection

The combustion side comes first: burner flame quality, heat exchanger integrity checked with a borescope camera and combustion analysis, venting inspected for blockages and corrosion, gas valve and manifold operation verified, and carbon monoxide readings taken at the furnace and at supply registers.

The electrical side follows: control board inspection, capacitors tested under load, wiring connections tightened, limit switch and rollout switch function verified, inducer motor and blower motor checked, and thermostat calibration confirmed against an accurate thermometer.

You get the findings in plain language with the measurements behind them, not just a pass or fail. Safety issues are explained with what was found and what it means. Call (571) 561-3454 to schedule before heating season.

The condensate drain gets attention on high-efficiency furnaces, since a clogged drain can shut the whole system down on a safety switch. The technician clears the trap and line, checks the drain slope, and confirms water flows freely. It is a small task that prevents one of the more frustrating no-heat calls: a furnace that is perfectly healthy but will not run because it cannot drain.

How Long Does a Furnace Last With Proper Maintenance

With annual professional maintenance, most furnaces serve 15 to 20 years. Without it, major failures often arrive years earlier. The difference is cumulative: clean flame sensors, tested capacitors, tight electrical connections, clear condensate drains, and a heat exchanger that never gets cooked by restricted airflow.

Maintenance also catches the slow killers before they finish the job: a heat exchanger stressed by chronic overheating from a neglected air filter, an inducer motor wearing against a partially blocked vent, a blower motor straining against dirty coils, a gas valve drifting out of spec.

No maintenance schedule makes a furnace immortal, but it moves failures off the January emergency calendar and onto a planned, budgeted timeline. That shift alone is worth the annual visit.

Keep the service record. A documented maintenance history supports warranty claims, gives future technicians a baseline to compare against, and tells a buyer the equipment was cared for if you ever sell the home. We leave a written report after every visit; file it with your other home records and bring it out when the next technician arrives.

When should I schedule maintenance?

Early fall, before the first sustained cold. September and October are ideal in Loudoun County.

How long does a tune-up take?

A thorough maintenance visit runs about an hour, longer if the technician finds something that needs attention.

Will maintenance lower my heating bills?

A clean, properly adjusted furnace runs closer to its rated efficiency. The bigger savings come from catching small problems before they become big repairs.

Do I need maintenance if the furnace is new?

Yes. Most manufacturer warranties require annual professional maintenance, and early visits catch installation issues while they are still easy to fix.

What is included in the safety check?

Combustion analysis, heat exchanger inspection, venting check, and carbon monoxide testing around the furnace and supply registers.
