Furnace Repair vs Replacement: How to Decide

Quick Answer

Consider replacing your Leesburg furnace when it is past 15 years, needs a major repair like a heat exchanger, or breaks down repeatedly. Compare the repair cost against replacement, factor in efficiency and reliability, and decide without pressure. Call (571) 561-3454 for an honest assessment of both paths.

Age Sets the Context

A 10-year-old furnace with a failed igniter is a repair. An 18-year-old furnace with a failed inducer motor, a history of breakdowns, and rising bills is a replacement conversation. Age does not decide alone, but it frames every other factor.

The Safety Trump Card

A cracked heat exchanger ends the debate. No repair makes a cracked exchanger safe except replacement of the exchanger or the furnace. When safety is on the table, cost comparisons take a back seat.

Adding Up the Real Costs

Beyond the quote, consider: how many repairs in the last two years, how much higher are winter bills than they should be, and how much is reliability worth on the coldest nights. A new furnace buys a decade-plus of not thinking about it.

Getting an Honest Assessment

We give you both numbers, the repair and the replacement, with plain pros and cons. No pressure, no countdown tactics. The decision is yours, and we respect it either way. Call (571) 561-3454 when you are ready to talk it through.

Questions About Furnace Repair vs Replacement

Is there a rule of thumb?

Multiply the repair cost by the furnace age. If it exceeds the replacement cost, replacement usually wins. It is a guide, not a law.

Can I replace in stages?

The furnace is one integrated system. Partial replacements rarely make sense except for specific warranty-covered parts.

How long does replacement take?

Most residential replacements complete in one day, with heat back by evening.
