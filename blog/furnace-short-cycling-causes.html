Why Your Furnace Keeps Turning On and Off

Quick Answer

A furnace that turns on and off rapidly is short cycling, usually from overheating, a faulty flame sensor, or an oversized unit. Short cycling wears parts fast and leaves rooms uneven. Check the filter first, then call (571) 561-3454 for diagnosis before the cycling damages expensive components.

Overheating: The Most Common Cause

When airflow is restricted, heat builds up inside the furnace until the high-limit switch trips. The burners shut off, the blower cools things down, and the cycle repeats every few minutes. Clogged filters, closed vents, and dirty blower wheels are the usual airflow thieves.

Flame Sensor Dropouts

A dirty flame sensor loses its grip on the flame signal intermittently. The furnace lights, the sensor drops the signal, the gas shuts off, and the sequence restarts. This version of short cycling often shows as the furnace trying three times and locking out.

Oversized Equipment

A furnace too large for the home satisfies the thermostat in minutes, shuts off, and restarts as soon as the temperature dips. This rapid cycling is built into the mismatch and only a properly sized replacement truly fixes it. It is also why load calculations matter at installation.

Why It Cannot Wait

Every start is the hardest moment in a furnace's life. Short cycling multiplies starts tenfold, hammering igniters, motors, and controls. What starts as an annoyance becomes a cascade of part failures. Call (571) 561-3454 at the first sign of rapid cycling.

Questions About Why Your Furnace Keeps Turning On and Of

How short is too short?

If the furnace runs less than five minutes per cycle repeatedly, that is short cycling worth investigating.

Will a new thermostat fix it?

Only if the thermostat is the cause. Most short cycling comes from airflow, sensors, or sizing, which a thermostat cannot fix.

Is short cycling dangerous?

It is hard on equipment and can indicate overheating, which deserves prompt attention before it cracks a heat exchanger.
