How It Works

From Your Call to Warm Air

What happens, in order, once you pick up the phone.

Step 1

You call, and a person answers.

Tell us what the furnace is doing and what you hear and smell. We will tell you what to check while a technician is routed.

Step 2

The visit is planned.

We confirm the address, the system type, and the symptoms so the technician arrives with the right parts and tests in mind.

Step 3

We find the cause first.

The thermostat call, inducer, ignition, flame, and blower are tested in sequence. The failed step points to the failed part.

Step 4

We fix it, then test it.

The repair is completed, the furnace runs through full heating cycles, and warm air is verified at the registers before we leave.

Symptom Checker

Tell Us What Your Furnace Is Doing

Match your symptom to see the usual cause and what a technician does about it.

No heat at all

What it usually means

Thermostat, power, igniter, or flame sensor failure.

What we do

We test the ignition sequence step by step to find the failed part.

Call right away if

The house is dropping temperature fast or it is below freezing outside.

Call (571) 561-3454 and describe what yours is doing.

Cold air from vents

What it usually means

Burners not lighting, or the high-limit switch tripping from overheating.

What we do

We check ignition first, then airflow and limit controls.

Call right away if

The furnace runs constantly but the house keeps cooling.

Call (571) 561-3454 and describe what yours is doing.

Furnace keeps turning on and off

What it usually means

Short cycling from restricted airflow, a bad flame sensor, or oversized equipment.

What we do

We measure airflow and test sensors to find the cycling cause.

Call right away if

Cycles last under five minutes and repeat all day.

Call (571) 561-3454 and describe what yours is doing.

Strange noises

What it usually means

Banging is often delayed ignition, whistling is airflow restriction, grinding is bearings.

What we do

We locate the noise in the heating cycle and trace it to the component.

Call right away if

The noise is loud, new, or getting worse quickly.

Call (571) 561-3454 and describe what yours is doing.

Burning smell

What it usually means

Dust burn-off on first fall run is normal; persistent burning is not.

What we do

We inspect for overheating components, electrical issues, or cracked parts.

Call right away if

The smell is strong, acrid, or paired with the CO detector sounding.

Call (571) 561-3454 and describe what yours is doing.

Thermostat seems wrong

What it usually means

Dead batteries, bad placement, loose wiring, or a failing thermostat.

What we do

We verify the thermostat signal reaches the furnace before touching the equipment.

Call right away if

The display is blank and the house is cold.
